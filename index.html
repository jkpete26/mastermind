How to play

Choose Play vs. computer or Two players. In two player mode, Player 1 sets a secret code, then passes the device to Player 2 to guess it. A color can appear more than once.

Making a guess

  1. Click a color below the board, then click a slot in the highlighted row. You can also press the number keys 1–6 to fill the next empty slot.
  2. Click a filled slot (with no color selected) to empty it. Backspace removes the last peg you placed.
  3. When every slot is filled, press Submit or Enter.

Reading the feedback

  • Black peg: a right color in the right slot.
  • White peg: a right color in the wrong slot.

The feedback pegs don’t say which slot they belong to. The text next to each row gives the same counts in words.

Colors and keys

Every color also has its own symbol, so you never have to rely on color alone.

    Difficulty

      Two players

      Player 1 selects a color and fills every code slot, then chooses Set code & pass to Player 2. After each guess, Player 1 enters the black and white feedback pegs. The computer checks that feedback against the secret code before showing it to Player 2.

      Hints

      A hint fills one slot in your current row with the correct color and locks it. That peg stays in place on later guesses. A hint never reveals the whole code.

      Score

      The timer starts when you place your first peg and stops when the game ends. Wins are ranked by fewest guesses, then fewest hints, then fastest time.
